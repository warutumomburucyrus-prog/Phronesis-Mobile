Privacy Policy for Phronesis

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Phronesis ("the app", "we", "our") is a study companion app for students. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Phronesis collects the following information, provided directly by you when you use the app:

  • Profile information: your name, university, and program, which you provide during setup.
  • Class schedule and unit data: extracted from timetable PDFs you choose to upload, or entered manually.
  • Notes you upload: PDF documents you add to generate summaries or quizzes.
  • Assignments: course names, topics, deadlines, and completion status you enter.
  • Quiz activity: topics studied and quiz results, used to calculate your familiarity and mastery scores.

How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used solely to provide the app's features to you:

  • Your timetable and notes are sent to Google's Gemini AI (via Firebase AI Logic) to extract schedules, identify topics, generate quizzes, and summarize notes.
  • Your assignment, schedule, and progress data is stored locally on your device using a local database, so it stays on your phone.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

Phronesis uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy practices:

  • Google Firebase (AI Logic, App Check) — for AI features and security verification. See Firebase's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — for managing subscriptions and purchases. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing — for processing in-app purchases.

Data Storage and Security

Most of your data (schedule, assignments, units, notes, quiz history) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. Content sent to Gemini AI for processing (timetables, notes, and quiz topics) is transmitted securely and is subject to Google's data handling practices for the Gemini API.

Your Choices

You can delete any assignment, unit, or note directly within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

Phronesis is intended for students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the contact information on the Phronesis GitHub repository.
